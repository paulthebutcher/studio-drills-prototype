# look-and-feel.md — Studio Drills (prototype)

> Thin by design. Paul gave no feel direction (S-01 §6: "No real idea. It should just be a
> progressive drill that improves skills over time."). Duolingo, Anki and Melodics were floated by
> the interviewer; Paul picked none. Nothing below is his stated preference unless marked.

## Adjectives

- Focused: one drill at a time, readable from across a desk while Logic is in front.
- Quiet: a practice tool, not a game.
- Precise: capability names exactly as Logic shows them.

## Anti-direction

- No streaks, XP, confetti, mascots or gamified pressure. *(Decision OD-9; Paul did not ask for them.)*
- No fake progress or invented stats on empty states.
- No templated AI-demo look (constitution §6).

## References

None supplied. Do not invent brand references.

## Hints (S-04 Q4)

- Each move has a small clickable tooltip-style trigger ("Hint") that opens a modal with the hint and the
  Apple guide link. Paul will tweak design and content later; keep it simple and replaceable.

## Voice (copy)

- Second person, plain, active: "Build a 4-bar hat pattern with velocity and chance variation."
- Moves say what to achieve in DAW-agnostic terms; Logic names appear only in the "In Logic Pro" line and hints.
- Empty states give direction: "Nothing to revisit yet. When a move gets you stuck, mark it and it
  shows up here."
- Use Logic's own names for capabilities (verify each against the Apple guide).
