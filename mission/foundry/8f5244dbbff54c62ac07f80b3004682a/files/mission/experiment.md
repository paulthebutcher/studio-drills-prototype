## Question and artifact

**Hypothesis (E-02, assertion):** constrained drills, sequenced so later drills combine earlier
techniques, get Paul to use production techniques that were new to him (in Logic Pro 12.4), and he can do
them again without the hint when a later drill calls for them.

**Artifact: new prototype** (OD-2). Why not the drill book alone: a text file tests whether single
drills are doable and interesting, but not the sequencing, the on-demand hints with recorded use, the
Revisit loop or the per-move record across sessions. Prerequisite: Paul checks Level 1 drill content in Logic
(Q2) while the rest of the app is built (Q1, answered 2026-10-26). If the app is not ready when the check
is done, run the same session plan from text and record results by hand; the decision rules still apply.

**Cannot establish:** that anyone else would want this; long-term retention beyond two weeks;
skill gains measured by anything other than Paul's own report. Paul is builder, operator and
participant, so every observation is self-reported (limitation L-1).

**Would make a build unnecessary:** if the Level 1 content check (Q2) shows the value is all in the
drill text and Paul is happy working from a list.

## Session

- **Session zero (before opening the app):** Paul writes, in `feedback/`, the last time he opened
  Logic and what he did, the Logic features he uses regularly, and three he knows exist but never use.
  This is the baseline for "new to me".
- **Task:** "Use Studio Drills whenever you want to practice in Logic over the next two weeks." No
  schedule, no reminders.
- **Scenarios:** Paul uses the practice database, which starts as `SD-ORD`. `SD-DEMO` and `SD-AWK`
  are for checking the app's behavior before and after, not for practice.
- **Observation targets (from the export):** dates of self-started sessions; per move, status, "new to me"
  and hint use; in Levels 2–3, whether moves on techniques Paul first met in Level 1 are done unaided;
  Revisit items cleared or stuck; content-wrong flags; notes.
- **Unaided vs prompted:** a move counts as unaided only when its status is "done unaided" and its hint
  was not opened (hint use is recorded automatically). Keep hint-assisted and unaided results apart in
  every reading.
- **Follow-up at two weeks:** which technique did he use in something that was not a drill? What
  did he verify in the guide that the drill got wrong? What did this replace (tutorials, noodling)?
- **Close:** pick the next decision from the table below. Enthusiasm is not a result.

## Decision rules

No numeric thresholds are set for a one-person study. Read the export against these patterns.

| Interpretation | What would be observed | Next decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Supports the hypothesis | Sessions started on separate days without prompts; several moves marked "new to me"; in Level 2–3 drills, techniques first met as "new to me" or "stuck" come back as "done unaided" with the hint unopened; Paul names a drill technique he used outside the app | Write Level 4+, then consider templates (Q5) or spaced re-quizzing |
| Contradicts the hypothesis | Paul stops after one or two drills with content flags or "not yet" outcomes; or most moves are "new to me = no" (too easy); or combined techniques stay "done with help", hint-assisted or "stuck" on return | Change the content approach (placement, smaller steps, different technique mix) before building more; or stop |
| Inconclusive | Too few sessions for life reasons; content flags dominate (the curriculum, not the format, failed); Level 1 not checked before use | Fix content (Q2) and rerun; do not extend the app |
